About this forecast

The points arithmetic is exact. What is uncertain is how the remaining races go, so the model rates every team and driver from this season's results (recent races count more), estimates how often each team retires, and simulates the rest of the season 50,000 times.

LIVE forecasts were saved before the race they predict. RECONSTRUCTED forecasts were rebuilt afterwards using only the data available at the time, so they cannot peek at the future, but they were not published in advance.

Backtested on every race since 2018 and checked on held-out seasons. Limits: teammates' retirements are modelled as independent, qualifying is not used, and eight past seasons is a small sample for judging season-level calls. Probabilities, not certainties.
